Power rankings

Who's actually good right now

Power score out of 100, the same formula every week. Results 25: win %. Recent form 20: all-play over the last three weeks (your weekly score against every other team). Season strength 20: all-play for the whole season, so a lucky record can't hide a weak team. Roster outlook 25: ESPN projection of your best possible lineup next week with players ruled out, on IR or suspended taken out, scored by how many of the other 11 rosters you out-project. Schedule ahead 10: how easy your next three opponents are, judged by their season all-play. Roster outlook and schedule use the same "how many teams do you beat" scale, so every part runs from 0 to its full weight. Ties go to points for. Arrows show the move from the week before.
